The modern sales tech stack

You don't need everything. You do need the right things for your stage.

CRM
HubSpot (free tier excellent), Salesforce (enterprise standard), Pipedrive (small business sweet spot), Close, Attio
Outbound
Apollo, Outreach, Salesloft, Lemlist, Smartlead, Instantly
Data & prospecting
LinkedIn Sales Navigator (essential), ZoomInfo, Apollo, Clay, Lusha
Meetings & calls
Calendly / SavvyCal for booking · Gong / Chorus / Fathom for call recording & AI analysis · Zoom / Google Meet
Proposals & contracts
PandaDoc, DocuSign, Proposify, Qwilr
E-commerce
Shopify (standard), WooCommerce, BigCommerce + Klaviyo for email/SMS
Email marketing
Klaviyo (e-com), ConvertKit / Beehiiv (creators), MailerLite, Customer.io
Analytics
GA4, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Hotjar (session recordings)
AI assistants
Claude (writing, analysis), ChatGPT, Gong AI (call summaries), Clay (AI-enriched outbound)
Rule

Tools amplify. They don't replace skill. A €0 stack with great fundamentals beats a €5K/mo stack with bad fundamentals every time.

Sales career ladder

Rough European market ranges for tech/B2B. Commission-heavy roles can exceed these substantially.

Year 0–2
SDR / BDR · Sales / Business Development Rep
€35–65K OTE
Year 2–5
AE · Account Executive
€60–150K OTE
Year 4–8
Senior AE / Enterprise AE
€100–250K+ OTE
Year 6–10
Sales Manager · Team Lead
€120–250K OTE
Year 8–15
Director of Sales · VP Sales
€180–400K+ OTE
Year 12+
CRO · Chief Revenue Officer
€300K–€1M+ OTE

Adjacent roles worth knowing

  • Sales Engineer — technical demos, deal architecture (often €150K+)
  • Customer Success Manager — post-sale relationship + expansion
  • RevOps — process, tools, data for sales team (high demand)
  • Sales enablement — training, coaching, content for sellers

The 90-day starter plan

If you finished this course and want to actually do the thing — here's a structure.

Days 1–30 · Foundation

Build the base skills before chasing leads.

  • Modules 0–2, daily
  • Read 2 books: Influence + To Sell Is Human
  • Pick your niche / product / vertical
  • Define your ideal customer in one sentence
  • Write your offer using $100M Offers framework
  • Build a simple lead-tracking sheet

Days 31–60 · Outreach

Start making real contact. Volume matters now.

  • Modules 3–6, daily
  • 50 prospect contacts/week minimum
  • Track conversation → meeting conversion
  • Record every call you legally can
  • Run 5 discovery calls/week
  • Get 3 demos / pitches done

Days 61–90 · Close

Convert the pipeline. Refine, double down on what works.

  • Modules 7–11
  • Run negotiation drills weekly
  • Close first deals — small ones count
  • Loss-review every failed deal
  • Implement basic onboarding sequence
  • Ask first customer for referral / testimonial
After 90 days

Audit. What worked? What didn't? Where's the leverage? Most beginners quit at day 60 — the ones who push through to day 90 have a real shot at building a career.

The metrics dashboard

Track these weekly. What gets measured gets improved.

  • Activity — calls made, emails sent, meetings booked
  • Conversion rates — at each stage of your funnel
  • Pipeline coverage — open pipeline ÷ quota (target: 3–4×)
  • Win rate — closed-won ÷ (closed-won + closed-lost)
  • Avg deal size — and trend over time
  • Sales cycle length — first contact to close
  • Forecast accuracy — what you committed vs. delivered
