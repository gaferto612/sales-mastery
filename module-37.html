37Commercial Acumen

Understand how the customer and your company make money.

Read business economics, design value-aligned packages, defend price with evidence, and recognize when a deal destroys value.

45 min lesson + practiceEvidence-led professional curriculumLevel 4
In this module
  • 1. Read the economic system
  • 2. Model unit economics
  • 3. Estimate willingness to pay
  • 4. Package around value
  • 5. Govern discounting
  • 6. Artifact: commercial model

1. Read the economic system

Understand revenue, gross and contribution margin, operating expense, cash flow, working capital, and capital expenditure. Identify which line your solution affects and when. A revenue claim is not profit; a cost saving is not cash unless resources actually change.

2. Model unit economics

For recurring models, examine acquisition cost, gross margin, payback, retention, expansion, support cost, and cohorts. For services or physical products, include utilization, delivery capacity, returns, inventory, and warranty. Define every denominator.

3. Estimate willingness to pay

Price is constrained by customer value, alternatives, budget, risk, and your economics. Use interviews carefully, analyze actual choices, run controlled tests where ethical, and examine price realization. Stated willingness to pay is weak evidence by itself.

4. Package around value

Choose a value metric customers understand, that scales with benefit, and that can be measured reliably. Separate tiers by meaningful needs rather than arbitrary withholding. Model edge cases, volatility, predictability, and gaming.

5. Govern discounting

Exchange price for term, scope, timing, payment, volume, or risk reduction. Record give/get, authority, and reason. Diagnose repeated discounts: weak positioning, poor qualification, quota pressure, packaging failure, or real market price.

6. Artifact: commercial model

Create a customer economics brief, value-metric assessment, packaging table, three-scenario price model, discount guardrails, and deal-level contribution analysis.
