35Account Strategy

Put scarce selling time where it can compound.

Prioritize markets and accounts, build relationship and opportunity maps, and run account plans as learning systems rather than static templates.

45 min lesson + practiceEvidence-led professional curriculumLevel 4
In this module
  • 1. Design the territory
  • 2. Prioritize transparently
  • 3. Create an account thesis
  • 4. Map influence and relationships
  • 5. Run a learning cadence
  • 6. Artifact: territory and account plan

1. Design the territory

Start with market potential, reachable accounts, capacity, travel or channel cost, product fit, and service constraints. Distinguish total addressable opportunity from the work a team can realistically cover. Rebalance using structural evidence, not a single missed period.

2. Prioritize transparently

Score fit, potential value, timing evidence, relationship access, competitive position, and cost to serve. Keep fit separate from engagement. Use tiers to change the service model, not to declare low-tier customers unworthy.

3. Create an account thesis

Write why the account might change, why now, where value could exist, which alternative is strongest, and what would disprove the thesis. Connect public strategy and operating signals to a specific workflow. Generic personalization is not insight.

4. Map influence and relationships

Map roles, priorities, power, stance, evidence, and relationship strength. Distinguish formal authority from expertise and network influence. Multithread to improve understanding and continuity, not to bypass a contact who said no.

5. Run a learning cadence

Map current outcomes to adjacent problems and credible capabilities. Review changes in evidence, not template completion. Every action needs an owner, date, hypothesis, expected signal, and next decision. Retire accounts when opportunity cost exceeds learning or value.

6. Artifact: territory and account plan

Deliver a capacity model, prioritization rubric, ten-account portfolio, account thesis, stakeholder map, opportunity tree, 30-day actions, and exit criteria.
