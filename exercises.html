—Exercises & Drills

Knowledge without reps is theater.

Twenty short, repeatable drills tied to the modules. Solo or with a partner. Recording yourself accelerates learning ~10×. Open each drill below for the prompt and scoring.

How to use this section

  • Solo: read the prompt, set a timer, write or speak the answer. Review honestly.
  • With a partner: trade roles (seller / buyer). Switch after each round.
  • In a group / class: rotate observers who give feedback after each round.
  • Recurring: the best drills get repeated weekly or monthly. Skills decay without use.
Read the book → close zero deals. Run the drills → close real ones.

Interactive Templates & Matrices

Use these fill-in-the-blank structures to build your own scripts before running the drills.

Subject: [Observation about their company, e.g., 'New office in Berlin']

Line 1 (Context): Saw that you just [Trigger event].

Line 2 (Problem): Usually when companies do that, they run into [Specific Pain Point] around month 3.

Line 3 (Solution/Value): We help [Role]s avoid that by [One sentence on how you solve it].

Line 4 (Ask): Worth a brief chat to see if this is relevant for you right now?

Pattern Interrupt: "Hi [Name], I'll be honest, I know you weren't expecting my call. Do you have 30 seconds for me to explain why I called, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"

The Hook: "I'm calling because I saw [Trigger]. Most [Role]s I speak to tell me they are struggling with [Pain 1] or [Pain 2]. Are you dealing with either of those right now?"

Objection Acknowledge & Mirror Isolate & Pivot
"It's too expensive." "Too expensive?" (Pause) "Aside from price, is there any other reason we wouldn't move forward?"
"We use [Competitor]." "You use [Competitor]. That makes sense." "What's one thing you wish they did better?"
"Send me an email." "I can absolutely do that." "Just so I don't send you generic marketing fluff, what specifically should I include?"

The 20 drills

Module 15 min prep · 30s delivery
You meet someone in an elevator who asks "What do you do?" — explain what you sell in 30 seconds so a 10-year-old would get it. Then so a busy CEO would care. Both under 30 seconds.

Score yourself:

  • Did you skip jargon?
  • Did you say what it does, not just what it is?
  • Did the listener want to ask a follow-up?
Module 115 min

Pick any 3 products near you. For each:

  • Write 3 features
  • Convert each to a benefit
  • Convert each benefit to a real-life outcome

Show your list to a friend. Which level did they react to most? That's your hint about which level to lead with for that product type.

Module 230 min

Find any sales page, ad, or email you've recently received. Identify which of the 7 Cialdini principles it uses. For each: where it appears, used well or poorly, one way to strengthen it. For each principle it doesn't use, whether it could and how.

Modules 2, 7Ongoing · 1 week

In every conversation this week (not just sales), label one emotion you sense in the other person, out loud:

"Sounds like you're frustrated." · "Seems like you're excited about that." · "It looks like something's bothering you."

Note what happens. People soften, tell you more, feel more connected. This single skill, practiced daily for a month, transforms how people respond to you.

Module 530 min

Pick a product. Write 20 questions sorted using SPIN:

  • 5 Situation questions
  • 5 Problem questions
  • 5 Implication questions
  • 5 Need-payoff questions

Eliminate the 10 weakest. The remaining 10 should be ones a buyer would actually want to answer.

Modules 7, 9Ongoing · 1 week

In your next sales conversation (or any conversation involving a decision):

  • Make your statement or ask
  • Count to 7 silently
  • Do not break the silence

Record what happens. Most sellers can't do this on the first try. Practice until normal.

Module 845 min

For your product:

  1. List the top 10 objections you've heard or expect to hear
  2. For each, write your 30-second response using the 5-step framework
  3. Practice each out loud until they flow

Refresh every 90 days. New objections emerge as your market evolves.

Module 7Ongoing · 1 week

In conversations, practice the Chris Voss combo:

  1. Mirror the last 3 words of what someone said
  2. Pause
  3. Label the underlying emotion
Them: "We just don't have the budget right now."
You: "Budget right now?" (pause) "Sounds like there's pressure on spending."

The amount of information you get from this in a single call is staggering once you practice.

All modules60 min

Find a published sales pitch (YouTube video, podcast interview, or real call recording). Map it onto:

  • The 7-step sales process — where are they?
  • Awareness level — what stage is the buyer at?
  • Cialdini principles used
  • Objections handled or unhandled
  • The close — direct? assumptive? other?

Identify the 3 strongest moves and the 3 weakest. Steal the strong ones.

Module 730 min

Pick any deal you're working on. Write down:

  • Your BATNA
  • Your reservation price
  • Your target price
  • The likely ZOPA
  • 5 variables you can trade besides price
  • The 3 most likely objections / tactics from the other side

Now imagine the negotiation playing out. Where would you push? Where concede?

Modules 5, 645 min

For one ideal customer, find 3 specific facts about them. Write 3 cold email variants:

  1. Personal angle — about them, their work
  2. Pain angle — about a problem you solve
  3. Curiosity angle — about something they'd want to learn

Get a peer to vote on which they'd reply to. Send the winner.

Module 630 min

Pick a product. Write 25 headlines using different formulas. Show all 25 to 3 different people. Note which ones get the strongest reaction (positive or negative — both are useful data). The bottom 20 die. The top 5 you test live.

Module 1110 min

Stand up. Practice saying out loud, with confidence:

"I don't think we're the right fit for you." · "I'd actually recommend you look at [competitor] — they handle this better." · "Let me not waste your time — let's stop here."

Most sellers feel resistance. That resistance is the skill you're building. The seller who can walk away closes more, not less.

Modules 8, 930 min · partner

Partner plays an aggressive, skeptical buyer. They start with one of:

  • "Your competitor is half the price."
  • "Send me a quote and I'll think about it."
  • "This isn't really a priority for us."
  • "We tried something like this before, didn't work."

You have 5 minutes to address it without getting defensive. Switch roles. Discuss after.

Module 415 min

Record yourself giving your sales pitch (audio). Listen back:

  • Where do you speed up? (usually = nervous)
  • Where do you trail off? (usually = unsure)
  • How often: "um," "like," "you know"?
  • Are you ending statements with rising tone? (sounds uncertain)

Re-record. Aim for: slower pace, downward tone on statements, zero filler words.

Module 10Ongoing

After every closed-won deal:

  1. Send a thank-you note (genuine, short)
  2. One week later, ask: "Who else do you know who might benefit from this? Happy to chat."

Track how many of your closed customers you actually ask. The number for most sellers is shockingly low — usually under 30%.

Module 1290 days

Pick ONE skill from this course. Just one. For 90 days:

  • Read everything you can find on it
  • Practice it daily in real conversations
  • Record yourself doing it weekly
  • Review the recordings
  • Get feedback from at least one other person

At day 90, your level in that skill will be visibly different. Then pick the next one. The single most reliable path to becoming a top seller.

All modules60 min · partner

You play the seller. Partner plays a buyer with a real situation (theirs). Run a full mock sales call:

  1. Greeting and rapport (5 min)
  2. Discovery using SPIN (20 min)
  3. Light positioning / pitch (10 min)
  4. Objection handling (10 min)
  5. Close attempt (5 min)
  6. Debrief (10 min)

Record it. Watch it back. Identify 3 specific things to do differently next time. Do this monthly. Your sales ability compounds.

Modules 8, 10, 1130 min

Find a buyer who said no to you. Interview them:

  • What was the deciding factor?
  • What would have to be true to change your mind?
  • Who did you go with? Why?
  • What did I do well? What could I have done differently?

Take it on the chin. Don't argue. Most sellers never do this. The ones who do level up 10× faster.

All modules60 min · quarterly

Every 90 days, ask:

  • Which module of this course am I weakest in?
  • What did I avoid this quarter that I should have done?
  • What lie did I tell myself about why a deal didn't close?
  • What would the best seller I know say I'm getting wrong?
  • What one thing will I drill next quarter?

Write the answers down. Compare to last quarter. Track your growth.
