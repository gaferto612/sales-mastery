Decoding MEDDPICC

Traditional frameworks like BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) are obsolete in modern complex sales. Elite organizations use MEDDPICC to relentlessly audit deal health and eliminate happy ears.

  • M - Metrics: The quantifiable economic impact of the solution.
  • E - Economic Buyer: The person who actually holds the discretionary budget and signs the contract.
  • D - Decision Criteria: The technical and business requirements the buyer is evaluating you against.
  • D - Decision Process: The exact steps the buyer takes to make the purchase.
  • P - Paper Process: The legal, security, and procurement journey (more on this below).
  • I - Implicate Pain: The severe business problem driving the urgency.
  • C - Champion: An internal seller who advocates for you when you are not in the room.
  • C - Competition: Who else (or what status quo) you are fighting against.

The Paper Process: Where Deals Die

The most devastating error an enterprise rep can make is celebrating a "verbal yes." In reality, a verbal yes simply means you have been granted permission to enter the Paper Process.

This involves Information Security (InfoSec) reviews, GDPR compliance checks, Data Processing Agreements (DPA), and brutal redlining from legal teams. If you do not uncover the Paper Process on Day 1, a deal you forecast for December will inevitably slip to March.

The Magic Question

"Once you decide we are the vendor of choice, what are the exact steps your legal and security teams require to execute a contract, and how many weeks does that historically take?"

Mutual Action Plans (MAPs)

A Mutual Action Plan (MAP) is the physical manifestation of your MEDDPICC strategy. It is a shared document (or digital hub) that outlines every single step required for the buyer to achieve their desired business outcome.

You do not frame the MAP as "how to buy our software." You frame it as "Your path to launching by Q3."

A strong MAP works backwards from their Go-Live date. If they need to be live on September 1st, and implementation takes 30 days, the contract must be signed by August 1st. If legal takes 45 days, the commercial terms must be agreed upon by June 15th. This mathematical reality forces the buyer to move at your pace without you ever having to be "pushy."

Driving Urgency

When a buyer stalls, amateur reps send "just checking in" emails. Elite reps use the MAP.

"Hi Sarah, to ensure we hit your September 1st launch date, we agreed that the security questionnaire needed to be completed by Tuesday. Since that's delayed, does the September 1st goal still hold, or should we push the entire timeline back?"

This holds the buyer accountable to their own goals, not your sales quota.
