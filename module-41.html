41Sales Organization

Build the environment in which good selling repeats.

Define work before roles, select with structured evidence, train through practice, coach observable behavior, and design incentives with safeguards.

45 min lesson + practiceEvidence-led professional curriculumLevel 5
In this module
  • 1. Design roles from customer work
  • 2. Use structured selection
  • 3. Onboard to verified performance
  • 4. Engineer performance
  • 5. Coach observable behavior
  • 6. Design incentives as a system
  • 7. Artifact: people system

1. Design roles from customer work

Map customer tasks, complexity, volume, expertise, geography, channel, and handoffs before choosing titles. Decide what to specialize and what to keep end-to-end. Every handoff creates coordination cost and potential context loss.

2. Use structured selection

Start with job analysis and observable competencies. Use the same work-sample questions and scoring anchors for every candidate. Combine structured interview, realistic simulation, reference evidence, and legal review. Do not select on charisma or “culture fit” alone.

3. Onboard to verified performance

Sequence market, customer, product, process, tools, ethics, and practice. Use retrieval, spacing, examples, simulation, feedback, and supervised work. Certify representative tasks—not content attendance.

4. Engineer performance

Diagnose knowledge, skill, motivation, role clarity, workflow, tool, capacity, management, or market gaps. Define the target behavior and outcome, then measure transfer after training. A content library is not a capability.

5. Coach observable behavior

Review one moment, ask for self-assessment, identify the decision, model an alternative, rehearse, and set an implementation intention. Separate coaching from forecast inspection so learning can be honest.

6. Design incentives as a system

Align pay with controllable contribution, customer value, time horizon, role interdependence, and capacity. Model thresholds, accelerators, caps, credit splits, clawbacks, windfalls, gaming, territory differences, and harm. Keep plans understandable and legally reviewed.

7. Artifact: people system

Deliver a role charter, competency scorecard, structured interview, work sample, onboarding map, coaching rubric, enablement experiment, and compensation-risk simulation.
