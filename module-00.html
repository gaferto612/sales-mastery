This course strips selling down to its mechanics, then rebuilds it across every modern context — from a corner shop to a €500K SaaS deal, from a TikTok product page to a kitchen-table negotiation.

Two myths to kill immediately

Myth 01

"Good products sell themselves."

No they don't. Better products lose to worse ones every single day because the worse ones were sold better. Distribution beats product, and selling is distribution at the human level.

Myth 02

"Selling is sleazy."

Bad selling is sleazy. Good selling is service. The difference is whether you'd be proud to tell the buyer everything you know about the deal. If yes, it's selling. If no, it's manipulation.

The sales mindset

Before any technique, you need three internal shifts. These are not motivational; they are functional. Without them the methods in this course produce mediocre results.

1. You are not asking for a favor

You're offering an exchange of value. If your product is good and the buyer needs it, you're doing them a service by closing — not the other way around. Sellers who feel guilty about asking for money fail. Quietly, slowly, but they fail.

2. Rejection is data, not identity

"No" almost never means "you are bad." It means: wrong person, wrong time, wrong message, wrong price, or wrong fit. Sellers who internalize rejection burn out. Sellers who treat it as a signal improve every week.

3. The buyer is more afraid than you

They're afraid of wasting money, looking stupid, getting it wrong. Your job is not to push — it's to reduce their fear. Calm, confident, and informative beats hype every time.

Selling is the transfer of belief over the bridge of trust. Build the bridge first. — The whole course in one sentence

A note on AI & selling in 2026

Selling has changed. Buyers research before they ever talk to you. AI writes the cold emails. Algorithms decide who sees your ad. The fundamentals in this course — trust, psychology, value, fit — matter more, not less, because everything around them is being commoditized.

Master the human layer and you'll outcompete tools forever.
