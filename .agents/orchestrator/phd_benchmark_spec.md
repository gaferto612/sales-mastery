# PhD-Level Course Module Benchmark Specification

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Baseline Modules Analyzed:** Module 27, Module 28, Module 29, Module 30 (Phase 7 Baseline)  
**Target Audience:** Audit Subagents, Course Content Authors, Quality Assurance Testers

---

## Executive Summary

This document defines the exact benchmark criteria for a **"PhD-Level Masterclass"** module in the Sales Mastery course. Based on a comprehensive audit of Phase 7 modules (`module-27.html` through `module-30.html`), a PhD-level module transitions enterprise sales instruction from superficial sales tactics to deep behavioral science, clinical psychology, neuroscience, and strategic cognitive agility.

Any module evaluated below this specification lacks the theoretical depth, physiological grounding, verbatim tactical dialogue, or structured clinical case evidence required by Phase 7 standards.

---

## 1. Benchmark Criteria Dimensions

### 1.1 Academic Foundations & Theoretical Rigor
Every PhD-level module must be anchored in peer-reviewed academic literature, foundational psychological frameworks, or recognized scientific theories.

- **Explicit Source Attribution:** Must credit seminal authors, founding researchers, publication years, and core monographs/papers.
- **Interdisciplinary Synthesis:** Must connect academic theories directly to B2B enterprise sales dynamics.
- **Baseline Examples:**
  - *Module 27:* Dr. Eric Berne, *Games People Play* (1964) — Transactional Analysis & Ego State Theory (Exteropsyche, Archaeopsyche, Neopsyche).
  - *Module 28:* Dr. Stephen Porges (1994) — Polyvagal Theory, Autonomic Nervous System, Ventral/Dorsal Vagal complexes, Neuroception.
  - *Module 29:* USAF Colonel John Boyd — OODA Loop (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act), *Schwerpunkt* (orientation focal point), military dogfight agility.
  - *Module 30:* Barkow, Cosmides, & Tooby, *The Adapted Mind* (1992) — Evolutionary Psychology, Kahneman & Tversky *Prospect Theory* (Asymmetric Loss Aversion), Ingroup/Outgroup bias.

### 1.2 Biological & Behavioral State Analysis
A PhD-level module rejects simplistic "buyer persona" descriptions in favor of biological, neurochemical, and somatic state mapping.

- **Autonomic Nervous System Mapping:** Explicitly identifies whether the buyer or seller is operating in Ventral Vagal (Safe & Social), Sympathetic (Fight/Flight), or Dorsal Vagal (Freeze/Shutdown) states.
- **Neurochemistry & Somatic Response:** Analyzes physiological indicators such as heart rate variability, vocal pitch tightening, adrenaline surges, cortisol spikes, mirror neuron firing, and micro-expressions.
- **Cognitive Dynamics & Ego Posture:** Diagnoses buyer ego postures (Critical Parent vs Nurturing Parent, Adapted Child vs Rebellious Child, Adult data-processor) and primal neural drivers (status hierarchy, survival metric, loss aversion).

### 1.3 Clinical Case Studies (Minimum 3 Per Module)
Every PhD-level module must contain **at least 3 real-world, clinical-grade enterprise case studies**.

- **Standardized Case Breakdown:**
  1. **Background & Context:** Specific deal size ($500K to $4M+ ARR/CapEx), industry (SaaS, Industrial Robotics, Cybersecurity, Enterprise Data, ERP), buyer persona/role.
  2. **The Incident / Trigger Event:** Concrete negotiation impasse, procurement trap, board objection, or buyer behavioral shift.
  3. **Psychological & Biological Reaction:** Contrast between amateur failure (regressing to Adapted Child/Sympathetic fight-flight) and elite success (Adult posture, Co-regulation, OODA re-orientation).
  4. **Quantitative & Qualitative Outcome:** Margin retained/lost, contract timeline, status shift.
  5. **Diagnostic Breakdown:** Step-by-step post-mortem explaining *why* the dynamic unfolded according to the module's theoretical framework.

### 1.4 Verbatim Roleplay Scripts & Tactical Annotations
Modules cannot rely on vague advice ("be confident"). They must provide word-for-word tactical dialogues.

- **Turn-by-Turn Scripting:** Complete buyer-seller exchanges showing the initial objection, the pattern interrupt, and the resolution.
- **Vocal & Physical Annotations:** Explicit guidance on vocal tone, cadence, pauses, and body language (e.g., `(Adult - maintaining steady, low vocal tone)`, `(Ventral Vagal - Grounded)`, `(Late-Night FM Voice)`, `(Seller pauses for 3 full seconds. Takes a slow, silent breath)`).
- **Amateur vs. Elite Contrast:** Side-by-side comparison showing how an untrained rep triggers a negative state versus how an elite rep forces an Adult/Ventral Vagal reset.
- **Deconstruction Section:** Detailed analysis following the script breaking down the psychological mechanisms at play.

### 1.5 Practical Toolkits, Checklists, & Troubleshooting
Every PhD module equips the rep with immediate diagnostic protocols and pitfall mitigation.

- **Troubleshooting Pitfalls Section:** Standardized Section 7 detailing failure modes (e.g., *Fake Adult/Hidden Parent*, *Physical Collapse*, *Faking Calmness*, *Ignoring the Freeze State*, *Confirmation Bias*, *Paralysis by Analysis*, *Misidentifying Status Threats*, *Fake Jargon*).
- **Actionable Pattern Interrupts:** Named, repeatable micro-tactics (e.g., *3-Second Pause Rule*, *Late-Night FM DJ Voice*, *The Shared Enemy Script*, *Schwerpunkt Re-Orientation*).

## 2. Technical & HTML/CSS Specification

### 2.1 HTML Structural Standards
Every PhD module follows a strict semantic document structure:

```html
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
<head>

  <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0">

  <link rel="stylesheet" href="styles.css">
</head>
<body>

<nav class="topbar">...</nav>

<header class="page-header">
  <div class="eyebrow"><span class="num">XX</span>[Module Category]</div>
  <h1 class="module-title">[Title with <em>emphasis</em>]</h1>
  <p class="lede">[2-3 sentence rigorous summary]</p>
  <div class="meta-strip">
    <span>[40-50] min read</span>
    <span>[Primary Domain] · [Secondary Domain]</span>
    <span>PhD Level Masterclass</span>
  </div>

<main>
  <aside class="sidebar">
    <div class="sidebar-title">In this module</div>
    <ul>
      <li><a href="#academic-foundation">1. Academic Foundation</a></li>

  </article>
</main>

<nav class="module-nav">...</nav>
<footer class="site-footer">...</footer>
</body>
</html>
```

### 2.2 Standardized 7-Section Architecture
Modules MUST contain 7 numbered `<section>` elements within `<article class="content">`:

| Section # | Required Anchor ID | Title Pattern | Core Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `academic-foundation` | `1. Academic Foundation: [Theory/Author]` | Author, historical context, seminal literature, core scientific principles, relevance to enterprise B2B sales. |
| 2 | `[topic-specific]` | `2. [The Core Model/Hierarchy]` | Deep breakdown of the primary model (e.g., 3 Ego States, 3 ANS States, OODA Loop phases, Status & Hierarchy). |
| 3 | `[topic-specific]` | `3. [Psychological Dynamics/Trap]` | Breakdown of common failure patterns (Parent-Child Trap, Neuroception, Schwerpunkt, Loss Aversion). |
| 4 | `[topic-specific]` | `4. [Applied Framework/Mechanics]` | Mechanics of state manipulation, co-regulation, fast-cycling, ingroup transition. |
| 5 | `case-studies` | `5. Clinical Case Studies` | Exactly 3 detailed enterprise case studies with background, incident, reaction, outcome, and post-mortem. |
| 6 | `roleplay` / `disrupting` | `6. Tactical Application: [Script/Reset]` | Verbatim dialogue transcripts with vocal/physiological annotations inside `.callout` containers and breakdown analysis. |
| 7 | `troubleshooting` | `7. Troubleshooting Pitfalls` | Bulleted list of failure points, cognitive biases, and physiological mistakes. |

### 2.3 Quantitative Depth & Density Requirements

- **File Size Range:** 12.5 KB – 17.0 KB of HTML code.
- **Word Count:** 1,400 – 2,000 dense, high-signal words.
- **Reading Time Meta:** Marked explicitly as `40 min read` to `50 min read`.
- **Badge Indicator:** Must include `<span>PhD Level Masterclass</span>` in the header `.meta-strip`.
- **CSS Styling Compliance:** Must utilize CSS classes from `styles.css` (`.callout`, `.callout-label`, `.sidebar`, `.page-header`, `.meta-strip`, `.eyebrow`, `.module-nav`).

## 3. Comparative Evaluation Checklist for Module Audits

Use this scorecard when auditing Modules 1 through 26 against the Phase 7 PhD Benchmark:

| Dimension | PhD Benchmark Standard | Gap Indicator in Lower-Tier Modules |
|---|---|---|
| **Academic Rigor** | Cited academic authors, foundational books, published peer-reviewed concepts. | Generic advice, unreferenced claims, pop-sales jargon without scientific backing. |
| **Biological Analysis** | ANS states, neurochemistry, somatic markers, vocal/vagal physiology. | Focus purely on verbal tactics without acknowledging buyer physiological state. |
| **Case Study Quality** | 3 enterprise clinical case studies ($500K-$4M ARR) with full diagnostic breakdown. | Abstract scenarios, zero financial numbers, shallow "before/after" stories, or < 3 cases. |
| **Roleplay Rigor** | Verbatim transcript with explicit vocal tone, pause duration, and posture notes. | Summarized dialogue ("tell the buyer X"), missing turn-by-turn breakdown. |
| **Formatting & Depth** | 7-section structure, sticky TOC sidebar, `.meta-strip` with "PhD Level Masterclass". | Fewer than 7 sections, missing sidebar TOC, basic inline layout, < 1,200 words. |
| **Troubleshooting** | Dedicated Section 7 identifying cognitive biases and physiological pitfalls. | Missing troubleshooting section or trivial top-tips list. |

## 4. Verification & Audit Trail

To verify compliance of any module against this spec:
1. Confirm presence of `<span>PhD Level Masterclass</span>` in `header.page-header .meta-strip`.
2. Verify all 7 required section anchors exist in `<aside class="sidebar">` and `<article class="content">`.
3. Count clinical case studies in Section 5 (must equal exactly 3).
4. Inspect Section 6 for verbatim transcripts inside `.callout` containers with tone/physiological annotations.
5. Verify scientific citations in Section 1 (author, year, monograph/paper).
