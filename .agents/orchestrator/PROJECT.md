## Interface Contracts & Criteria Standard
Every module evaluation systematically scores and analyzes:
1. **Academic Foundations & Theoretical Rigor**: Peer-reviewed citations, empirical research, psychological/cognitive theories (Berne, Porges, Boyd, Kahneman & Tversky, Barkow/Cosmides/Tooby, Sweller, Bandler & Grinder, etc.).
2. **Behavioral & Biological State Analysis**: Autonomic Nervous System state mapping (Ventral Vagal vs Sympathetic vs Dorsal Vagal freeze), neurochemistry (cortisol, dopamine, oxytocin), somatic indicators, Transactional Analysis Ego States.
3. **Clinical Case Studies**: Enterprise sales scenarios ($500K-$4M+ ARR) with 5-part diagnostic breakdowns.
4. **Verbatim Roleplay Scripts**: Word-for-word dialogues with vocal tone, pause duration, and physiological annotations in `.callout` boxes.
5. **Practical Toolkits & Section 7 Troubleshooting**: Pitfall mitigation, named pattern interrupts.
6. **Structural Formatting & Depth**: 7-section architecture, sticky TOC, `PhD Level Masterclass` badge, 1,400-2,000 words, 12.5-17.0 KB file size.
