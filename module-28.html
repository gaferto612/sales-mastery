The biology of trust.

The Three Nervous States

The human autonomic nervous system operates in one of three primary states:

  • Ventral Vagal (Safe & Social): The buyer feels safe. They are creative, open to new ideas, and capable of rational, Adult-to-Adult negotiation.
  • Sympathetic (Fight or Flight): The buyer feels threatened. They become aggressive (demanding discounts) or evasive (canceling meetings, ghosting). Logic shuts down.
  • Dorsal Vagal (Freeze): Extreme threat. The buyer shuts down completely. This is the "No Decision" zone (the JOLT effect). They are paralyzed by the fear of making a mistake.

Neuroception in Sales

Neuroception is the subconscious process where our nervous system constantly scans the environment for cues of safety or danger. It happens below conscious awareness.

When you jump on a cold call and your voice is overly enthusiastic, pitched high, and speaking rapidly, the prospect's nervous system instantly detects a "threat" (a predator trying to extract resources). They immediately shift into a Sympathetic state (Fight/Flight) before you have even finished your pitch. "I'm not interested, please take me off your list."

Co-Regulation

Human nervous systems sync with one another. If you are anxious, the buyer becomes anxious. If you are deeply calm, the buyer's nervous system will eventually mirror yours. This is called Co-Regulation.

The Late FM DJ Voice

To co-regulate a stressed buyer, use what Chris Voss calls the "Late Night FM DJ Voice." Drop your pitch, speak slowly, and use a downward inflection at the end of your sentences. This sends a biological signal of safety directly to their brain stem, bypassing their logical defenses.

You cannot logic a buyer out of a Sympathetic state. You can only co-regulate them out of it through your own physiology.

The Power of the Pause

Silence is the ultimate tool for nervous system regulation. When a buyer raises a hostile objection, the amateur seller's heart rate spikes, and they immediately start talking to fill the void.

The elite seller pauses for 3 full seconds. They breathe. This pause proves to the buyer's subconscious that the seller is not under threat. By the time the seller responds with a calm, low voice, the buyer's hostility has already begun to dissipate.
